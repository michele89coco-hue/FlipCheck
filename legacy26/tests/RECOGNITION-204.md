# Pokémon raw: build 204

Clear photographed subject, printed number (including No.006), printed year and language bind candidate selection and all searches. Catalogue HP, illustrator, attacks and speculative product text cannot veto four agreeing photo keys. Number, name, year and language conflicts remain disqualifying even after an image comparison. Photo and catalogue provenance are separate.

The lower-band copyright provides the last explicitly printed four-digit year; it is not silently replaced with a catalogue release year. An explicitly documented printing year can differ from the set release year. Uncertain repeated OCR/vision readings do not become clear by repetition. A catalogue in another language is a discovery reference, not the photographed edition.

The PriceCharting product adapter extracts attributed card records and images already present in downloaded pages. Discovery leads from other sites remain provisional. Japanese/Chinese name translations preserve V/ex/etc. Existing images are tried before another paid search. No per-card identity table or increased scan budget.

Band symbols accept literal star/diamond/First Edition values. Generic reflective and specific holo/reverse readings are compatible; actual opposing finishes remain unresolved. New band responses provide panel-local coordinates, transformed by the app to original-photo coordinates. Existing recorded responses without panel_role retain their original coordinate semantics.

TCGdex name searches read up to three 100-card pages within the existing deadline, with incomplete coverage reported. Reference: https://tcgdex.dev/rest/filtering-sorting-pagination
